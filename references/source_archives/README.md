## Scientific map

- Volume 0 defines the algebraic/geometric architecture and a limited early
  topology language.
- Volumes I--III define regulated light-front foundations, common nucleon GTMD
  overlaps, and dynamical Wilson lines.
- Volumes IV--VII develop spin-1 nuclear dynamics, matching/evolution,
  inference/validation, and a concrete microscopic nucleon Hamiltonian.
- Volumes VIII--X introduce the symmetry-adapted tensor-network prediction
  compiler, dynamical gluon Fock sectors, and sea/chiral/PCAC antiquark
  structure.
- Volumes XI--XV develop nonzero-transfer GTMDs, second-order Wilson structure,
  and resolved nuclear sectors including NN-pion, DeltaDelta, six-quark, and
  hidden-color contributions.
- Volumes XVI--XVIII define scheme-qualified TMD ensembles, resolved evolution,
  process qualification, and small-b operator-product matching.
- Volumes XIX--XXI define source-qualified process inputs, reproducible bridge
  geometry, and regulator-specific TMD operators with soft matching.

Volume XVI is the missing source bridge between the microscopic M1 layer and
process-ready observable construction. It records common quark/gluon
Collins--Soper evolution, rank-aware Bessel transport, small-b matching,
resolved nuclear evolution, covariance propagation, process-readiness
certificates, and explicit accuracy manifests. Its all-order equations are
formal identities and closure targets. The volume expressly does not claim a
completed all-order evolution, a physical extraction, complete T-odd matching,
or physical process predictions.

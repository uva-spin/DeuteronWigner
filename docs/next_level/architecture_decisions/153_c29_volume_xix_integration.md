# ADR 153: Integrate Volume XIX without evidential promotion

## Decision

`references/volume_xix_source_qualified_process_inputs.tex` is the
authoritative formal contract for source-qualified process inputs, physical
covariance gates, joint-member semantics, and data-ready observable bundles.
Its supplied bytes are preserved and content addressed. Each of its 50 stable
requirements is mapped to inherited source-process evidence or a C29
bridge-level enforcement/fail-closed record.

The presence of the formal source does not promote any distribution, process,
physical-input, inference, or production capability. A formal requirement is
not a numerical finite scheme adapter, a scheme-qualified microscopic TMD
vector, an exact fixed-order/asymptotic partner, or a joint cross-root measure.

## Consequences

- Volume XIX is no longer listed as missing in current C29 records.
- Historical C25--C28 absence statements remain explicitly historical.
- Volume XX was missing at this decision's original checkpoint; ADR 154 records
  its subsequent authoritative integration.
- C30/B1 remains the next implementation package.

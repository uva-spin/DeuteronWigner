# C22/M3 unresolved physics gaps

These are required unavailable boundaries, not optional enhancements:

- Sivers/Qiu--Sterman, Boer--Mulders, genuine g1T and h1L-perp, f- and d-type
  tri-gluon, and tensor-polarized T-odd channels require their own multiparton
  operator bases and source-audited twist-three matching/evolution.
- Spin-1 gluon double-helicity flip requires a reconciled project-scheme TMD
  coefficient and collinear splitting kernel.
- Pretzelosity has a zero twist-two coefficient through the represented order;
  nonzero physical content requires the appropriate higher-twist operator.
- Full N3LO executable status requires deterministic ingestion and independent
  validation of the papers' complete expressions/ancillaries. C22 preserves
  those papers but executes only the explicit order-one blocks in source.
- Pion-active, transition, DeltaDelta, compact six-quark, and other many-body
  operators require operator-specific coefficients unless common-local-
  operator universality is proved.
- A physical nonperturbative Collins--Soper covariance bundle and controlled
  large-b model remain unavailable; C22 cannot launder these C21 limitations.
- Process qualification, hard/fragmentation inputs, Glauber certificates, and
  W+Y belong to the subsequent Volume-XVII package.

# C81/IFCONTACT3 — fail-closed public composition audit

C81 authenticates C78 and C80 through their public APIs. C78 publishes symbolic `KAPPA` coordinate domains and explicitly marks projected coefficients `NOT_EVALUATED`; C80 evaluates only a caller-supplied raw coordinate and does not publish the required total C78-coordinate map. There is therefore no legal product `c_kappa K_kappa`, bound, or M-squared entry to aggregate. No sparse or matrix-free contact matrix is constructed.

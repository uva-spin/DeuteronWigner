# C29 unresolved physics and bridge gates

C29 does not find a complete numerical cross-root bridge. This is the correct
fail-closed result of the identity audit, not a missing implementation shortcut.

The rank-zero proton quark and antiquark families still require a complete
finite adapter between the ART25 UV/rapidity/soft/Fourier conventions and a
source-qualified microscopic TMD bundle. The microscopic root presently
provides regulated operator identities and validation-level matching/evolution,
not a physical scheme-qualified numerical proton TMD vector.

The quark Collins-Soper comparison requires compatible derivative,
perturbative-subtraction, rapidity, and large-b conventions plus microscopic
kernel covariance. A quark bridge cannot be copied to gluons: the adjoint
kernel and gluon-link/color identities remain distinct.

DY and SIDIS one-leg bridges remain unavailable because the common numerical
target-side operator and complete scheme adapter are absent. The C28 route is
also W-only and has no source-identical fixed-order/asymptotic partner. The C23
analytic Y is a different validation identity and is not substituted.

Phenomenological deuterium records have no complete adapter to the resolved
microscopic deuteron graph. NN-only is diagnostic scope, not matched total.
NNPI, DeltaDelta, six-quark cluster, hidden color, transition/interference,
coherent pilot, and total matching remain explicit unresolved gates.

T-odd quark/gluon and multiparton families remain unavailable. Unknown scheme,
matching, Hamiltonian, Fock, Wilson, nuclear, partner, target, large-b, and
missing-Y discrepancies remain nonzero-unknown and have not been fitted.

Volume XIX and Volume XX formal source files are absent from the repository.
Their absence is hash-audited and no content is inferred for them.

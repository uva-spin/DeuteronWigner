# Missing finite-basis vacuum soft-sector calculation

C33/S0 must define a vacuum/eikonal sector compatible with the C11
longitudinal and transverse regulator identity without changing the historical
C11 state.

It must construct the four-line fundamental Wilson soft operator with the same
future/past geometry, transverse closure, path ordering, modified-delta
rapidity prescription, b-space measurement, and color normalization as the
C32 collinear operator. It must specify whether K, Nmax, bHO, x_min, boundary
conditions, and the zero-mode policy act on eikonal/vacuum modes and derive the
corresponding vacuum basis or a proved regulator conversion.

At one loop it must calculate Wilson-line exchange, individual-line self
energies, cusp/endpoints, UV and rapidity singularities, gauge dependence, and
the soft-region measurement. It must construct the matching collinear
zero-bin with the same regulator, demonstrate the missing- and duplicate-
subtraction residuals, and establish inverse-square-root allocation exactly
once.

Only after that sector exists may the remaining microscopic self-energy,
vertex, real, staple, instantaneous, Hamiltonian/basis-counterterm, endpoint,
zero-mode, and mixing graphs be combined. The resulting object must close UV,
rapidity, common-IR, gauge, mu/zeta anomalous dimensions, state independence,
and the three-point C7 basis trajectory before any TMD export.

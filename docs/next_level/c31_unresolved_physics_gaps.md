# C31 unresolved physics gaps

1. The C11 operator lacks a source-qualified UV, soft, zero-bin, and rapidity
   renormalization calculation in its actual finite-basis regulator.
2. No theorem proves equivalence of the C11 regulator to a regulator covered by
   the continuum, LaMET, or lattice matching literature.
3. The operator-identical partonic difference and its instantaneous,
   endpoint, Hamiltonian-counterterm, and Wilson contributions are uncomputed.
4. The formal Volume-XVI project TMD has not been numerically constructed from
   C11; C19--C22 remain validation layers.
5. Consequently there is no microscopic renormalized u/d/ubar/dbar vector,
   no TMD-specific regulator convergence trajectory, and no cross-root
   distribution diagnostic.
6. Large-b, Fock/Wilson, basis/TTN, evolution, and external-model remainders
   remain separate and mostly nonzero-unknown.

Continuum project-to-ART25 convention alignment is source supported for an
already-renormalized input, but does not resolve any of items 1--4.

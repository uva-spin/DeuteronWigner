# C8 unresolved physics gaps

C8 deliberately stops at a finite valence-only Hamiltonian EFT benchmark.
The following are requirements for later packages, not zero contributions:

- explicit \(qqqg\), \(qqqq\bar q\), higher-Fock, zero-mode, and higher-OAM
  basis sectors;
- canonical quark--gluon dynamics, instantaneous fermion/gluon partners,
  regulator gauging, and sector-dependent counterterms;
- Ward--Takahashi closure beyond the reduced vector-charge benchmark;
- regulator and basis towers large enough for controlled extrapolation;
- a demonstrated renormalization trajectory and continuum/scaling window;
- physical mass, current, axial/tensor, and form-factor calibration with
  uncertainty propagation and frozen validation data;
- nonzero-transfer parton correlators, GTMD overlaps, microscopic gauge-link
  transport, and soft/rapidity matching;
- gluon and sea distributions, complete OAM decomposition, and microscopic
  phase generation;
- nucleon-to-deuteron matching, nuclear currents and consistent spin-1
  helicity matrices;
- LF-to-QCD scheme matching, evolution, process factorization, and inference;
- overlap subtraction before combining explicit \(qqqg\) dynamics with the
  H1 induced color-spin image.

The H1 confinement coefficients accelerate infrared convergence within the
declared basis and are refitted at each resolution. They are not a universal
immutable QCD potential. The TTN benchmark establishes a valid truncation
axis only for these small \(qqq\) blocks.

The next work package, C9/H2, must address the dynamical \(qqqg\) sector,
sector-dependent renormalization, instantaneous partners, Ward closure, and
the microscopic interface to C5/C6 without connecting an incomplete state to
production.

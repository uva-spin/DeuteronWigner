# ADR-008: Alternative and replacement groups

Status: accepted for C2

Composition plans select at most one member of an alternative group.
`REPLACES`, `EXCLUDES`, benchmark-only status, non-central sensitivity status,
and duplicate physical identity are enforced before numerical evaluation.
Ensemble members and wave-function choices are alternatives, never additive
central mechanisms. Gluon f/d and future/past channels require later
process-level selection rather than canonical addition.

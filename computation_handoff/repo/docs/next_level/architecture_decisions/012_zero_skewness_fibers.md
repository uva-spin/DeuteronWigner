# ADR-012: Zero-skewness momentum fibers

Status: accepted for C3

Incoming, average, and outgoing light-front fibers are immutable identities.
They share plus momentum, invariant mass, normalization, regulator, Hilbert
space, and sector; their transverse momenta are `-DeltaT/2`, zero, and
`+DeltaT/2`. C1 `DELTA_T` is mandatory. Nonzero skewness or `Delta+` fails
closed.

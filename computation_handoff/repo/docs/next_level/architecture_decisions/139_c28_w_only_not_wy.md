# ADR 139: W-only evidence cannot imply W+Y

Status: accepted.

ARTEMIDE v3.01’s executed ART25 route returns the low-qT resummed W term. Full
W+Y requires a fixed-order cross section and the fixed-order expansion of W
with identical process, states, measurement, cuts, scheme, masses, scales,
thresholds, rank, harmonic, and perturbative order. The analytic C23 Y oracle
is not a substitute and no parameter retuning may conceal a mismatch.

# C15 unresolved physics

N0 is restricted to the nucleonic NN sector. NNPI, DeltaDelta, compact
six-quark and hidden-color sectors require new normalized amplitudes and
co-matched operators. Coherent shadowing, antishadowing, tagged final-state
interactions, and nuclear Glauber propagation require amplitude-level
rescattering rather than scalar corrections. The two-body current is only an
analytic co-matching benchmark; a complete exchange-current and partonic
two-body operator basis remains absent. UV/link-shortening, rapidity/soft
matching, Collins--Soper evolution, process factorization, and inference are
also unresolved. None is represented by zero physics or a fitted ratio.

# 1. Authority policy for C77 and later packages

C77 must apply the following authority hierarchy.

## 1.1 External primary-source authority is required for

```text
a physical QCD operator or action not already fixed in the project;

a regulator or subtraction scheme whose definition changes the
scientific object;

a claim of regulator identity or scheme equivalence;

a published phenomenological value, fit, ensemble, covariance, or
experimental input;

a perturbative coefficient, counterterm, or matching relation not
derived in the project;

a physical normalization that cannot be fixed by the already
declared project conventions.
```

A famous or prominent source is not sufficient merely because it exists.
It must match the relevant operator, regulator, normalization, and scope,
or it remains a comparison or method oracle.

## 1.2 Exact project-derived authority is sufficient for

```text
a mathematical consequence of already source-locked equations;

basis-state enumeration from already fixed quantum-number rules;

canonical row, column, block, and global identifiers;

a deterministic ordering convention;

a matrix-orientation convention and an exact adapter to historical
storage;

a local-to-global basis crosswalk;

n_CM and m_CM labels derived from the exact relative/CM basis
constructor;

hashes, serialization, inventories, and public APIs;

an exact composition of already authenticated operators.
```

These objects do not require an external paper. They are project-owned
mathematical and implementation authority once they are derived
deterministically, independently checked, and frozen.

## 1.3 Explicit project model choices are allowed when

```text
the underlying physics is underdetermined;

no unique external authority exists;

the choice is necessary to define an exploratory finite-basis model;

the choice is labeled PROJECT_DEFINED or MODEL rather than
SOURCE_DERIVED or PHYSICAL;

alternative choices and sensitivity/holdout tests remain visible.
```

Do not fail closed merely because no paper has chosen the project's exact
identifier, serialization, truncation label, or internal basis ordering.

## 1.4 A no-go is required only when

```text
the missing choice changes the physical scientific object;

the requested claim requires source or regulator identity that is
not established;

or two exact internal derivation routes are genuinely inconsistent.
```

C77 must not search for an external paper to supply global basis IDs,
row/column names, CM labels, or matrix orientation. It must derive them.

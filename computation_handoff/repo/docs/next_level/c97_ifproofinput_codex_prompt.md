
operand record from exact commit-pinned source and frozen semantic objects.

The central scientific rule is:

> **The proof result must not participate in construction, identity,
> canonicalization, or authentication of the proof input.**


the input is frozen, as an independent regression holdout.

historical result, when two independent recovery routes disagree, or when
the recovered input does not reproduce the frozen C90 result.

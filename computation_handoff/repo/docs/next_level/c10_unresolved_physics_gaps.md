# C10 unresolved physics gaps

The model lacks continuum extrapolation, complete higher Fock sectors,
zero-mode closure, a physical pion sector, full chiral and non-Abelian Ward
proofs, matched GTMD/PDF/TMD operators, soft/rapidity subtraction, evolution,
nuclear composition, and inference. Sea asymmetries and spin ledgers are
finite-basis, regulator-dependent predictions. The next package is C11/H4:
microscopic nonzero-transfer quark, antiquark, and gluon GTMD helicity
matrices, complete T-even projector closure, local-current/OAM consistency,
and replacement of the analytic C3/C4 common-parent pilots.

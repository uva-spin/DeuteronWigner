## Definition of a real math/physics blocker

You may enter `REAL_MATH_PHYSICS_BLOCKER` only when all relevant lawful
routes have been exhausted and at least one of these is established:

1. **Absent indispensable authority**  
   A required scientific object has no exact repository/Git/local-source
   authority, no exact identified official source, and cannot be derived from
   the authenticated project action, conventions, constraints, and operators.

2. **Exact derivation contradiction**  
   Two independently valid, source-qualified derivations disagree after
   exhaustive sign, unit, orientation, representation, boundary, scheme,
   source-order, and convention audits.

3. **Mathematical inconsistency**  
   The identity/constraint system has a certified nonzero exact compatibility
   or left-null residual. Mere rank deficiency or underdetermination is not an
   inconsistency.

4. **No lawful physical identification**  
   A Hamiltonian- or observable-relevant parameter remains necessary but
   cannot be fixed, matched, or proven irrelevant after every authenticated
   source-side identity and physical condition is exhausted.

5. **Nonexistent required map**  
   The required finite-basis, matching, boundary, or quantum-interface map is
   proven not to exist at the declared scope, rather than merely not yet
   implemented.

6. **Continuation would require fabrication**  
   Progress would require inventing a formula, source, numerical value,
   equivalence, counterterm, null representative, physical convention, or
   unsupported zero.

Before declaring this blocker:

- run two independent read-only scientific audits;
- run one provenance audit;
- identify the smallest exact missing or contradictory object;
- record all attempted routes;
- prove why another implementation job cannot lawfully resolve it;
- create `/Users/dustin/work/DeuteronWigner-yolo/state/REAL_BLOCKER.json` conforming to the supplied

- update `AUTOPILOT_STATE.json` to `REAL_MATH_PHYSICS_BLOCKER`;

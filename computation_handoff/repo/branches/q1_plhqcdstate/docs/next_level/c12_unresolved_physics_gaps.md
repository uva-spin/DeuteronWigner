# C12 unresolved physics and next package

C12 completes the declared first-order validation boundary but leaves these
physics requirements fail-closed:

- explicit qqqgg active-gluon intermediate states;
- explicit qqqq-qbar-g antiquark intermediate states;
- second and higher Wilson orders and Dyson/Magnus convergence;
- continuum-QCD UV matching and finite scheme coefficients;
- a complete continuum soft function and rapidity renormalization;
- Collins-Soper and renormalization-group evolution;
- process hard factors, fragmentation, and qualified gluon color weights;
- nuclear spin-1 composition and correlated nuclear covariance;
- inference and production promotion.

The active-gluon and antiquark first-order routes therefore carry
`INDUCED_OPERATOR_SUPPORTED_WITH_REMAINDER`, not explicit-sector
completeness. The precise replacement task is C13/H6: add qqqgg and
qqqq-qbar-g sectors, validate their color/permutation/Fock coupling, replace
the induced H5 channels, and test second-order Dyson/Magnus convergence.

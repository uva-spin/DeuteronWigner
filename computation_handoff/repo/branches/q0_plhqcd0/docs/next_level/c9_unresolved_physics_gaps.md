# C9 unresolved physics gaps

C9 does not contain light-sea sectors, higher gluon sectors, zero modes,
complete instantaneous kernels, continuum extrapolation, full non-Abelian
Slavnov--Taylor identities, matched field-strength correlators, physical cut
support, or controlled soft/rapidity matching. Its canonical spin ledger is
finite-basis and unmatched. The reduced vertex and induced confinement remain
Hamiltonian-EFT benchmark terms.

The next package is C10/H3: fully antisymmetrized light-sea sectors, chiral
dynamics, axial/PCAC currents, explicit/induced sea subtraction, and
positive-\(x\) microscopic antiquark exports.

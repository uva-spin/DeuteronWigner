# ADR 036: Finite cut and overlap two-cells

**Decision:** Extend the pilot provenance with explicit
`EQUIVALENT_COUNT_ONCE`, `OVERLAP_SUBTRACT`, `ALTERNATIVE_TO`, `ADDS_TO`, and
`REMAINDER_OF` relations.

**Reason:** Numerical equality cannot determine whether contributions are
duplicate, independent, alternative, or unresolved.

**Status:** Executable finite subset; not the general Volume 0
`Provenance2Complex`.

# C30 unresolved physics gaps

The following requirements remain physically unresolved rather than set to
zero:

1. A renormalized, soft-subtracted, rapidity-qualified microscopic proton
   rank-zero TMDPDF for u, d, ubar, and dbar.
2. A cited finite conversion from that microscopic definition to the exact
   ART25 regulator, UV, rapidity, soft-factor, threshold, and scale convention.
3. An order-matched inverse, round-trip test, RG and rapidity consistency test,
   and quantified truncation remainder for that conversion.
4. Same-operator convergence sequences in resolution, Fock content, Wilson
   order, solver accuracy, Fourier quadrature, and TTN bond dimension.
5. A numerical microscopic covariance or explicitly justified deterministic
   error model that is not confused with an assumption ensemble.
6. Quantified matching, evolution, large-b, and omitted-Fock discrepancies.

Until items 1--4 are supplied, the bridge cannot calculate a meaningful
distribution residual. Until items 5--6 are supplied, it cannot support
statistical compatibility, calibration, or inference. ART25 members and
microscopic states remain distinct provenance roots with no joint member
measure.

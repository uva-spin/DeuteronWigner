# ADR 041: Center-of-mass factorization policy

**Decision:** Require explicit CM quantum labels, factorization residuals, and
Lawson intrinsic-level drift diagnostics.

**Reason:** A truncated oscillator basis can mix intrinsic and CM motion;
nominal basis labels alone do not demonstrate factorization.

**Status:** Gate implemented for C7 reference blocks. Larger interacting
towers must revalidate it.

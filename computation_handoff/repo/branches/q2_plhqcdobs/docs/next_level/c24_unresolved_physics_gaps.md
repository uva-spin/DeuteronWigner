# C24 unresolved source and physical-input gaps

C24 completed the source audit without inflating qualification. No process
chain passes every source gate and no physical covariance-qualified bundle is
consumed.

The exact Zenodo ARTEMIDE 3.01 archive is preserved and verified, but it does
not contain the ART25 constants or its declared 500 replica files. The model
list explicitly says the generated MSHT20 replicas can be shared by request.
Consequently ART25 central/member semantics cannot be reconstructed and the
archive cannot source-qualify either a two-hadron DY boundary or the joint
TMDPDF/TMDFF SIDIS boundary.

The CS papers are preserved, but no compatible machine-readable covariance or
joint-member bundle has been ingested. Their interfaces are comparison/source
records, not physical boundaries. A quark result is not copied to gluons and
nonperturbative Casimir scaling is not imposed.

MAPFF, the independent pion/kaon FF analysis, and HAPS are paper-audited only.
No official local member set is bound to ART25's TMDFF members, so a collinear
FF is never relabeled a TMDFF. CuTe-MCFM 10.3 and the SIDIS N3LO numerical
ancillaries are likewise not locally locked; high-order W+Y is unavailable.

Inclusive b1 lacks the complete operator-specific coefficient, target-mass,
higher-twist, and heavy-flavor source plan. Tagged DIS lacks a qualified
spectral/pole/FSI adapter and tagged-to-inclusive oracle. The NN assumption is
kept distinct from the unavailable full matched deuteron total.

Heavy-pair DIS remains conditional because the exact process soft/color
adapter, compatible gluon TMD boundary, and rank-two source benchmark do not
close. All T-odd and multiparton channels remain unavailable.

The next package must obtain the official ART25 model bundle (constants and
all 500 correlated members) and implement deterministic ARTEMIDE 3.01 DY and
SIDIS benchmark adapters. It must not substitute a newer release.

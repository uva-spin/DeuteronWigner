# C23/P0 unresolved physics gaps

- Source-qualified and physical-input process sets are empty. Exact sourced
  hard/FO/fragmentation bundles and compatible covariance-bearing CS/large-b
  inputs are required before either tier can execute.
- Inclusive b1 needs its operator-qualified collinear tensor inputs,
  coefficient/target-mass conventions, and an eligible nuclear plan.
- Tagged DIS needs its target-fragmentation spectral, pole, FSI, acceptance,
  and operator-specific qualified inputs; it is not ordinary SIDIS.
- Rank-one and rank-three process harmonics have analytic mathematical W/Y
  oracles but no eligible operator inputs in the current minimal set.
- T-odd Sivers/Qiu--Sterman, Boer--Mulders, genuine worm gears, f/d tri-gluon,
  and tensor-polarized T-odd channels require their own multiparton matching
  and evolution.
- NNPI, DeltaDelta, six-quark cluster/hidden-color, transition/interference,
  coherent, and matched-total nuclear process terms remain unavailable.
- Conditional gluon processes require source-qualified process-specific hard,
  soft/color, heavy-mass or jet, and factorization/Glauber inputs for stronger
  claims. Colored-hadroproduction universal-TMD products remain broken.

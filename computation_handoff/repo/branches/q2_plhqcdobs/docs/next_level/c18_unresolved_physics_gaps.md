# C18 unresolved physics gates

C18 does not determine physical DeltaDelta, compact-six-quark, or hidden-
color probabilities. Their amplitudes and the reported diagnostic weights
remain regulator-, resolution-, and representation-dependent.

The six-quark-only Fock truncation has direct quark operators but no direct
antiquark support and only induced gluon support with a visible remainder.
The Delta and six-quark parents lack light-front-to-QCD UV, rapidity, soft,
and small-b matching. No Collins-Soper evolution or process factorization is
applied. The coherent calculation is an analytic helicity pilot, not a
physical diffractive/Glauber shadowing calculation. Complete chiral EFT and
continuum non-nucleonic dynamics are also outside the N3 scope.

These gates motivate C19/M0: common-scheme light-front-to-QCD operator
matching for the complete microscopic nucleon and deuteron parent.

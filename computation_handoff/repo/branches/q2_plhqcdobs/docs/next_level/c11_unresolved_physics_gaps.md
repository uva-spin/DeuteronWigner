# C11 unresolved physics and closed gates

C11 completes the declared regulated H4 validation scope, not a physical
QCD prediction. The following items are intentionally unresolved and fail
closed:

- physical staple-shaped Wilson transport and absorptive spectral support;
- quark/antiquark T-odd amplitudes and gluon f-type/d-type pole channels;
- continuum, UV, rapidity, and soft-factor matching of the finite basis;
- common rank-aware QCD evolution;
- a Hamiltonian-owned independently validated local tensor current;
- nonzero skewness, ERBL overlaps, zero modes, and off-diagonal Fock maps;
- correlated nuclear covariance, nuclear soft/phase completion, and spin-1
  nuclear composition of the microscopic nucleon parent;
- fragmentation, hard/process maps, W+Y matching, likelihood, and posterior;
- strange and heavy active flavors;
- a continuum-limit proof and canonical-to-kinetic OAM matching.

The finite H3 transverse/longitudinal basis is the actual computational
representation presently available. C11 exposes its mode, grid,
quadrature, derivative, bond, and truncation errors separately; it does not
silently extrapolate it to continuum support.

The next executable package is C12/H5: microscopic Wilson-line dynamics on
these H4 helicity matrices, including physical spectral support and typed
quark, antiquark, and gluon link-odd projections. Matching and evolution
remain a later package.
